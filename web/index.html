Signal

Five things worth knowing today, picked for what you're building.

Signal is a personal brief agent. It reads what you follow and what you don't, keeps only what matters for your work, posts five learnings to Slack, and changes tomorrow's brief based on what you 👍 or 👎.

See the latest briefs View the code

How it works

1 · Gather
Three lanes of sources

Each lane fetches on its own, so one failing never blocks the brief.

2 · Dedupe
Nothing twice

Anything sent in the last 14 days is dropped, using a table in Neon Postgres.

3 · Curate
A ruthless curator agent

A Mastra agent ranks candidates against your profile and past votes, and can only cite links it was given.

4 · Deliver and learn
Slack, with buttons

Each item has a learning, why it matters to you, and one action. Your votes steer the next brief.
